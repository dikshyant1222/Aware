Small gestures. A first step.

This is an experimental text-to-avatar prototype with a rigged human model and articulated fingers. It recognizes 14 common ASL words and phrases, including “thank you,” and fingerspells other words. It preserves English word order and does not translate English grammar into ASL or support Nepali Sign Language.

The 3D motion engine animates individual finger joints, J and Z movements, and repeated-letter bounces. The avatar has not been validated by a Deaf ASL expert and is not an authoritative teaching or interpreting resource.

Motion engine: three.ws (Apache 2.0). Rendering: Three.js (MIT). Avatar: Microsoft Rocketbox (MIT), adapted with a model-specific hand rig and refined surfaces. Engine license · Avatar license.
